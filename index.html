The short version

NEXUS does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. There is no server, no account and no analytics. Every NEXUS feature runs on your device.

One thing this policy will not pretend about

NEXUS is a tool you use to send things to other people's services. It would be dishonest to describe it as if nothing ever leaves your computer.

When you press Mask & Send, the sanitized text is submitted to the website you are using — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or wherever you were typing. That is the entire purpose of the button. What NEXUS changes is what is in that submission: the detected values are replaced with placeholders such as [API_KEY_REDACTED] before it goes. The same applies when you copy sanitized text and paste it somewhere, or download a redacted screenshot and share it.

So, precisely:

NEXUS transmitsnothing, ever — no detection service, no telemetry, no account
You transmitwhatever you choose to send, after NEXUS has sanitized it

Once a submission reaches a third-party website, that site's own privacy policy governs what happens to it. NEXUS has no control over, and no visibility into, what they do with it. It also cannot protect anything you send without using it — pressing Allow Once, or typing into an application NEXUS is not running inside, sends the original.

What NEXUS processes

To do its job, NEXUS reads:

  • Text you are about to submit on websites where it is active, so it can scan it before you send it.
  • Images you give it, in the Screenshot Scanner, so it can recognise text in them and cover what is sensitive.
  • Clipboard text you hand it, either by pasting it into the Clipboard Guardian or by pressing "Read clipboard & scan" after granting the optional permission.
  • The hostname of the page you are on, so it can classify the destination and apply your policy.

All of this is processed in memory, on your device, and then discarded.

What NEXUS stores

On your device only, using chrome.storage.local:

  • Your settings and policy — thresholds, destination rules, trust levels, custom patterns, which sites you have disabled.
  • An audit log of metadata — for each scan: a timestamp, the entity types found and how many of each, a risk score, the destination hostname, and which action you took.

The audit log never contains your content, a detected value, a masked preview of a value, or the position of a detection within your text. That is enforced in code: every event passes an assertion that rejects anything credential-shaped before it is written, and the test suite checks it.

What NEXUS never does

  • It never sends your content anywhere to be classified. There is no cloud detection service, no remote API and no model download at runtime.
  • It never uploads a screenshot. Text recognition runs from a WebAssembly engine bundled inside the extension.
  • It never transmits clipboard content.
  • It never writes your content to a log.
  • It never uses chrome.storage.sync, which would copy settings to your Google account.
  • It contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising.

Network activity

NEXUS makes no network requests. The extension's content security policy pins connect-src to 'self', so its pages cannot reach an external origin. Every feature works with the network disconnected, and the test suite verifies that by severing the network and running the product.

Permissions

  • storage — Stores your settings, your policy and the metadata-only audit log on this device using `chrome.
  • activeTab — Grants access to the tab you are on ONLY at the moment you click the NEXUS toolbar icon.
  • clipboardWrite — Writes the sanitized text back to your clipboard when you press "Sanitize & copy", and writes an empty string when you press "Clear clipboard".
  • clipboardRead (optional, off by default) — OPTIONAL, and not granted at install.

NEXUS requests no host permissions and does not use <all_urls>.

Your data, your control

  • Export your audit log as JSON from the extension's settings page.
  • Delete the audit log, or reset every setting, from the same page.
  • Uninstall the extension and everything it stored is removed with it.

Children

NEXUS is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Limitations, stated plainly

NEXUS reduces the chance of accidentally disclosing sensitive information. It does not eliminate it:

  • Detection is based on patterns, structure, entropy and context. It misses things, and it occasionally flags things that are not sensitive.
  • Optical character recognition misses text that is small, blurred, stylised or low-contrast. Text it does not recognise is not redacted.
  • NEXUS protects the surfaces it runs on. It cannot protect an application it is not running inside.
  • It is not a guarantee against data loss, and nothing in this document should be read as one.
